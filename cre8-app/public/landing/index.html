Questions

What can a fund hold?

Tokenized US stocks, crypto, and stablecoins that earn by lending. Each fund lists exactly which ones, and the contract refuses everything else.

Who holds my money?

The fund contract. The creator and the fund’s plan can only trade within the rules. Only holders can withdraw, and only their own share.

When can I leave?

Any time. Withdraw as USDT, or take your share of the fund’s assets directly.

What does it cost?

No yearly management fee. Every fund shows its fees before you deposit. The creator’s fee is set at launch and is taken only from profit.

Can I use it today?

CRE8 runs on BNB Chain testnet today, and the funds on this page are examples. Join the waitlist in the app to try it.

What are the risks?

Prices can fall and you can lose money. Contracts, price feeds and the markets a fund trades on can fail. Nothing here is investment advice.
